# Anticoagulation at the end of life with cancer: a service for North Wales

A 10-minute presentation and a bedside decision aid for antithrombotic therapy in people with cancer near the end of life, proposing a shared service (oncology, palliative care, haematology, specialist nursing, pharmacy, district nurses and GP) with MDT review, family meetings, discharge planning and anticoagulation stewardship.

**Live page:** https://abdulmannan1974.github.io/serenity/

## What is in this repository

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The presentation, with the decision aid built in. Served by GitHub Pages. |
| `CAT-EOL-Anticoagulation-Service-North-Wales-STANDALONE.html` | The same file, to download and open offline by double-clicking. |

## Offline and private by design

- One self-contained HTML file. Fonts are embedded; it works without internet.
- It stores nothing and sends nothing. A Content Security Policy in the file tells the browser to block every network connection. Data typed into the decision aid disappears when the page is closed.

## Using it

- Slides: arrow keys or swipe; **F** full screen; **N** speaker notes.
- Decision aid: press **Open the decision aid**; **Back to the presentation** or **Esc** returns.

## Evidence

NICE NG89, NG158 and NG31 (recommendation wording checked on nice.org.uk, 11 October 2026); the SERENITY programme publications (Horizon Europe grant 101057292) and website; Gurumurthy et al. 2026 (Res Pract Thromb Haemost 10:103459, Figures 1 and 2 redrawn under CC BY 4.0); RHESO, HIDDen and Chin-Yee 2022; EHA 2022 guidance on antithrombotics in thrombocytopenic patients with cancer; Hokusai VTE Cancer, SELECT-D and Caravaggio. PMIDs were verified against PubMed. Full references are on the final slide.

## Important

- This is a teaching and pathway-development aid. It is **not a validated clinical tool or score** and does not replace clinical judgement, specialist advice or local policy. Evidence on stopping antithrombotics at the end of life is observational.
- This is an independent service proposal. It is **not affiliated with or endorsed by the SERENITY consortium**; it cites their published work.
- The example patient in the decision aid is fictional. No patient data is included.
